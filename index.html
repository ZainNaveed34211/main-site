What types of projects do you typically work on?

Kluzar builds professional business websites, company dashboards, web applications, and 3D and digital experiences for companies worldwide. Every project is built for the web and focused on the client's business goals.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timing depends on the requirements of each website or application. After you send an enquiry through the contact page, the Kluzar team reviews it and discusses the scope and timing with you before work begins.

What's your development process like?

You send an enquiry with your business details and project goals. The Kluzar team reviews it, discusses the requirements with you, then designs, builds, and launches your website on its own subdomain, and manages it afterwards.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Kluzar manages your website after launch, including website care, minimum changes on request, and support through the support page and info@kluzar.com. The exact support included depends on your plan, and plan details are on the payment subdomain.

Can you work with our existing systems and team?

If you already have a website, content, or business tools, mention them in your enquiry and the team will discuss what can be carried over or connected. Anything that cannot be confirmed in advance is discussed after enquiry.
