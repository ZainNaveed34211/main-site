How we work

Build, launch, manage — in that order

Every product above went through the same four steps we use for client work. Nothing on this page was rushed out as a demo and left behind.

01

Build

We design and build the site or product properly first, with security considered from the start rather than added at the end.

02

Launch

The product goes live on its own subdomain, checked in a real browser before anyone is asked to look at it.

03

Manage

After launch we keep it running: updates, security, SEO, posting and social visibility, so it stays useful instead of going stale.

04

Improve

Real use shows what needs fixing. We keep improving the products based on what actually happens, not on what we guessed at the start.

How we work

A Kluzar project starts with the business, not with a template. We ask what the business does, who its customers are and what the website needs to do for them, and the design follows from those answers. A finished site is delivered on its own subdomain, launched and then managed — SEO, posting, social visibility, reviews, security and updates — so the business owner does not have to become a web developer to stay online.

Security comes first in all of our coding. Inputs are validated before they are used, access is verified before protected data is served, and entitlements are checked on the server rather than trusted from the browser. That approach is visible in the products on this page: the accounts system uses multi-factor authentication, and the tools run client-side so nothing a visitor checks is uploaded anywhere.

We do not force a template onto a business. Templates are a starting point at most; the layout, the content and the structure are shaped around what the business actually needs. And we do not publish fake data or fake features: if something is not built yet, we say so, and if a number is a demo, it is labelled as one.
