Company dashboards & SaaS

A company dashboard that brings employees, work, customers and orders into one clear place — built by Kluzar and managed for your business.

What a company dashboard is

The Kluzar company dashboard lives at dash.kluzar.com. It is a single place where a business manages itself: employees and departments, projects and tasks, calendars, documents and reports, together with a clear view of customers and orders. Instead of work living in different spreadsheets, chat threads and notebooks, it sits in one system that everyone involved can see and follow.

That matters most when a business grows past the point where one person can hold everything in their head. A task assigned in a chat message is easy to miss; a deadline written on a notebook is easy to forget; a customer enquiry answered by one person is invisible to everyone else. When the work is in a dashboard, it can be seen, followed up and finished — and the owner can see what is happening without asking each person one by one.

One view of the whole business

The dashboard is organised around the way a company actually works. People are grouped into departments, work is grouped into projects and tasks, and calendars, documents and reports sit alongside them. The owner sees the state of the business at a glance, and each person sees the work that belongs to them. Nothing depends on remembering where a file was saved or who was told what in a message last week.

Built with the same care as the websites

The dashboard follows the same approach as a Kluzar website: designed around the business that will use it, built to be clear and easy to use, and handled with a security-conscious approach. Access is verified before protected data is served, and entitlements are checked on the server rather than trusted from the browser, so what a person can see and do in the dashboard is decided by the system, not by what their screen happens to show.

Who it is for

The dashboard is for businesses that have outgrown running everything from memory, spreadsheets and chat messages. If you manage a small team and work keeps slipping through the cracks — a task nobody follows up, a deadline nobody sees, a customer nobody replies to — a single place for people, work and customers makes the difference between guessing and knowing.

It is also for businesses that already have a Kluzar website and want the inside of the business to be as organised as the outside. The website brings customers in; the dashboard keeps the work behind those customers under control.
