Frequently asked questions

Answers about what Kluzar builds, how pricing works, plans, support, security, accounts, and where we are based.

What does Kluzar build?

Kluzar creates digital products, websites and business-focused digital solutions for companies worldwide, including professional business websites, UI/UX design, company management dashboards, web applications, and 3D and digital experiences.

How does website pricing work?

Website pricing is based on your requirements and is given as a custom quote after you send an enquiry through the contact page. There is no fixed price for a website build.

What do the subscription plans cover?

Kluzar offers three plans: Starter (free), Growth ($10) and Max ($25). They cover services such as SEO, website security, reputation analysis, website changes for Kluzar-built websites, social management, and monitoring tools. Full plan details and subscription processing are handled on the payment subdomain at payments.kluzar.com.

How do I pay for or manage my subscription?

Plans and subscription processing are handled on the payment subdomain at payments.kluzar.com. After payment, you return to the Kluzar site you came from.

What happens after my website launches?

After launch, Kluzar manages your website afterwards. This includes website care and minimum changes on request, such as text changes and photo replacement. The exact level of after-launch management depends on your plan, and details are shown on the payment subdomain.

How do I contact Kluzar?

You can send an enquiry through the contact page, email info@kluzar.com, use the support page at support.kluzar.com, or chat on WhatsApp at +92 328 5049744.

What is the Company Management Dashboard?

The Company Management Dashboard at dash.kluzar.com is a Kluzar service where a business can manage its company, employees, projects and work in one place. Which plans include it is shown on the payment subdomain.

How does Kluzar handle security?

Kluzar builds with a security-first approach: services are implemented with security in mind, accounts use secure sign-in, and access is checked on the server before anything premium is served. Kluzar does not claim certifications it has not earned.

Do I need a Kluzar account?

If you use a Kluzar service that needs sign-in, you sign in through the accounts service at accounts.kluzar.com. Some services, such as a basic website scan or a public page, do not need an account.

Where is Kluzar based and who does it serve?

Kluzar is based in Sialkot, Pakistan, and serves businesses worldwide.

Still have a question?

Send an enquiry through the contact page and the Kluzar team will reply to you.
