About Kluzar

A website agency & SaaS from Sialkot, Pakistan

Kluzar builds, launches and manages websites, dashboards and digital tools for businesses without one — serving businesses worldwide, from Sialkot, Pakistan.

Who Kluzar is

Kluzar is a website agency and SaaS from Sialkot, Pakistan, serving businesses worldwide. It was founded for a simple reason: most businesses still have no website, and the ones that do often have one that nobody maintains. A business without a website is hard to find, hard to trust and hard to contact — and the owner usually has no time to fix that alone.

So Kluzar does the work for them. We build a finished website, launch it on its own subdomain, and then manage it: SEO, posting, social visibility, reviews, security and updates. The client does not edit the site themselves by default — minimum changes are done by Kluzar on request — because the point of hiring an agency is that the business owner gets on with running the business.

02

Why we build our own products first

Before asking any business to trust us with its website, we built our own products and put them live: 43 free tech tools that run entirely in the browser, a company dashboard for premium users, an email outreach product, a blog, an accounts system and a payments page. These are not mockups or concept designs. They are systems Kluzar runs every day, and they are the proof of how we work.

Building our own products first changes how we build for clients. The tech tools taught us how to make something fast and useful without asking visitors for an account or an upload. The dashboard taught us what a business owner actually wants to see — content, orders and bookings, customers, reviews, posts and analytics in one place — and what only gets in the way. Outreach taught us how email really behaves: warmup, DNS setup, deliverability and the difference between a mailbox people trust and one they ignore.

That experience means client work does not start from zero. When we build a website for a business, the security approach, the structure and the management routine already exist, because they are the same ones our own products run on. And when something in our own products breaks or confuses someone, we fix it there first — so the lesson is learned on our systems, not on a client's.

It also keeps us honest. A product that is live has nowhere to hide: if the tools are slow, if the dashboard is confusing, or if a page does not load, anyone can see it. We would rather be judged on systems that are running today than on promises about what we might build tomorrow.

Our values

What we stand by

Six things that guide every website we build and every product we run. They are simple to state and harder to keep — which is why they are written down here.

Value

Security first

Security comes first in all of our coding. Inputs are validated before they are used, access is verified before protected data is served, and entitlements are checked on the server rather than trusted from the browser. It is a habit, not a feature to add later.

  • Validate everything before using it
  • Verify access before serving data
  • Server-side checks behind what the screen shows
Value

No fake data or features

We do not publish fake data, fake features or invented results. If something is not built yet, we say so. If a number is a demo, it is labelled as one. A business should be able to trust what it sees on its own website — and what it sees on ours.

  • Nothing invented to fill a page
  • Demos clearly labelled as demos
  • Honest about what is not built yet
Value

Real products before promises

We build our own products first and run them live, so clients get systems that are already proven in daily use. The tech tools, the dashboard and Outreach exist today — they are the evidence, not the pitch.

  • Our own systems, running live every day
  • Lessons learned on our products, not on clients
  • Judge us on what is there, not on what we say
Value

Premium, modern design

A website is often the first thing a customer sees, so it should look like the business takes itself seriously: modern, premium and clear, with proper animation and attention to detail. Design is not decoration here — it is how a business earns trust in the first few seconds.

  • Modern and premium, never generic
  • Clear and easy to understand
  • Built for the first impression
Value

Clear pricing

Pricing is stated plainly: Starter is FREE, Growth is $10 and Max is $25. Website builds are custom quotes, because the scope of one business is never the same as another — but the quote is given before work starts, and what it covers is spelled out.

  • Starter FREE, Growth $10, Max $25
  • Website builds quoted individually
  • What the price covers, spelled out upfront
Value

Managed, not do-it-yourself

By default, the client does not edit the site themselves. Minimum changes are done by Kluzar on request, and the ongoing management — SEO, posting, social visibility, reviews, security and updates — is part of the service, not a task handed back to a busy owner.

  • Changes done by Kluzar on request
  • Ongoing management after launch
  • The owner runs the business, not the website
